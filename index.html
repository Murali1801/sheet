<bottom-sheet> · Web Component · MIT

A bottom sheet that moves like the one in your phone.

Flick it and it lands where the flick was heading. Velocity projection, a real spring, rubber banding and nested scroll in one custom element. No dependencies, 4.1 kB gzipped.

npm i @murali1801/sheet

Drag the yellow grabber. Flick it. Pull it past the top. The readout shows why it lands where it does.

Tune and copy

Every change applies to the live sheet and rewrites the tag below.

Nested scroll

            
At 50% a drag on the list moves the sheet. At 100% the list scrolls, and pulling down from its top brings the sheet down.

</bottom-sheet>

    How the physics works
    
          
    The same rules iOS uses, in about 100 lines of pure functions with unit tests.
    
        
    
        
      
          
    1. Velocity
      Pointer events with capture. Only the last 100 ms of samples count, so a finger that rests before lifting does not fling.
      2. 
          
    2. Projection
      lands = y + v · d / (1 - d), d = 0.998/ms
      A 1 px/ms flick travels 499 px. The sheet goes to the detent nearest that point, not the one you let go next to.
      3. 
          
    3. Dismiss
      A downward flick over 0.5 px/ms from the lowest detent, or a projection that ends below half the lowest detent. Higher up, a flick lands on a lower detent instead.
      4. 
          
    4. Rubber band
      offset = (1 - 1 / (x · 0.55 / dim + 1)) · dim
      Past the top, the sheet follows less the further you pull, and never more than the screen height.
      5. 
          
    5. Spring
      Semi-implicit Euler in 240 Hz sub-steps on each frame. Stiffness 400, damping 40, mass 1 is critically damped. Release velocity seeds the spring, so there is no seam.
      6. 
          
    6. Nested scroll
      Below the top detent, content drags move the sheet. At the top, the content scrolls, unless it is at scrollTop 0 and the finger moves down.
      7. 
          
    7. Keyboard
      visualViewport tells the sheet where the keyboard starts. It lifts above it and opens fully when a field inside has focus.
      8. 
          
    8. Motion and access
      Transforms only, no layout reads per frame. Reduced motion makes every snap instant. Modal sheets use showModal() for focus and Esc; the grabber is a button.
      9.
